## Decisions
Use a valid theme-color mix for selection rather than interpolating vd3 computed channel expressions into rgba(). Preserve native textarea selection/copy behavior. Normalize getUserMedia errors at the microphone boundary with actionable text; an application cannot invent microphone devices when the browser exposes none. Keep permission requests before model loading.

Bulk updates stay within existing dependency major versions; do not remove or relax age/trust/build policies. Refresh affected transitive dependencies and apply narrow patched-version overrides where necessary. Review any unpatched advisory separately; do not remove useful lint tooling just to suppress audit findings. Development branches are used for sibling edits.
